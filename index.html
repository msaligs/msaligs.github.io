About Me

I am driven by curiosity more than convention.

My work revolves around AI systems, automation, computer vision, Linux, infrastructure, and the invisible layers that keep technology functioning reliably. I enjoy understanding systems deeply — not just using them, but dissecting how they behave under pressure, failure, and scale.

But my interests do not stop at machines.

I am equally drawn toward communication, psychology, language, and the patterns behind human behavior. To me, the most interesting problems exist where technology and people intersect.

I learn by building, breaking, observing, and rebuilding. Most of what I know was shaped less by linear paths and more by experimentation, persistence, and an obsession with understanding how things actually work beneath the surface.

I don’t just study machines. I study systems — technical and human alike.

For me, growth is not about collecting titles. It is about becoming more precise, more aware, and more capable — technically, intellectually, and personally.
